FAQ

Good to know

Is this a medical website?

No. This design is for research information and catalog presentation only. It does not provide medical advice, treatment claims, dosing, injection instructions, or human-use guidance.

Can real products be added?

Yes, after the product information is verified and appropriate for lawful research-only presentation. Regulated or prescription items should not be turned into direct-purchase storefront listings.

Can the colors and branding be changed?

Yes. The color palette, logo treatment, typography, catalog cards, and contact details are all easy to update.
